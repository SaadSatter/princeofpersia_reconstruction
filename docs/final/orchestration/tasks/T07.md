Design:
1. **Encoder.** It maps one seat's observation, plus a bounded history the controller keeps itself, to a fixed-length float vector. Only what a human sees. No figure ids; order-invariant over figures (for example the K nearest by distance, then by observed fields). Document every feature.
2. **Actions.** The macro vocabulary, with a mask of macros currently possible (RIG_TRAP masked until traps exist; keep the slot).
3. **Reward.** Terminal +1 win, −1 loss, 0 draw. Optional small shaping (hearts dealt or taken, disguise acquired), every term documented and switchable.
4. **Trainer.** PPO in numpy: MLP 2×64 tanh, actor and critic, GAE. Opponents: the scripted baseline plus earlier policy snapshots. Seats swapped every episode.
   - Train **only on tuning starts**. The held-out H6 starts belong to the Evaluation Specialist (T08) and are not available to you; say so in RL_POLICY.md.
   - Time-box training: total wall time at most 90 minutes on 2 CPU cores, using background processes and polling, since one shell call is limited to 10 minutes.
   - Fixed seeds. Log every update (steps, mean return, win rate vs baseline on tuning starts) to `tools/rl/logs/`. Stop at the time cap; do not pick a checkpoint by score.
5. **Policy.** Weights are exported to JSON, with a SHA-256 recorded in RL_POLICY.md and checked at load. `agents/rl/policy.py` does inference in pure Python (no numpy), argmax by default. A parity test checks it equals the numpy forward pass to 1e-9 on 200 recorded observations.
Acceptance (failing tests first):
- encoder leak test: identical features for a disguised rival and a real guard with the same observed fields; no hidden key used;
- order invariance;
- mask correctness;
- deterministic rollouts for a seed;
- the inference parity test;
- the weight hash is checked at load;
- a short smoke training run (≤60 s) in CI-safe tests;
- the full training run is logged, with its commit, wall time, steps and final tuning-start results against the baseline, all reported truthfully, including if the policy is weak;
- full suite green;
- Handoff to QA and Evaluation.
