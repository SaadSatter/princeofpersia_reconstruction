Design constraints:
- A `SeatController` protocol: `decide(observation: dict, legal: tuple[str, ...]) -> str` plus `name`, `kind` (`human` | `scripted` | `policy` | `fallback`), optional `reset(seed)`. Controllers receive **deep copies** of their own seat's observation and legal list only; never the episode, the duel, the other seat or the seed.
- `VersusRunner(episode_factory, controllers={"A":..., "B":...}, decision_every=6, limits=...)`: ticks the episode; at each seat's decision tick asks its controller; validates the answer against the current `legal_actions(seat)`; illegal or malformed answers are refused, counted per seat, and replaced by `WAIT` labelled `source:"fallback"`; a controller exception or a decision slower than `timeout_s` (default 5 s, measured with an injectable clock) gives a labelled `WAIT` fallback without stopping the ticks of the other seat.
- Per-seat limits: max decisions, max fallbacks (terminal reason when exceeded), duel limit from the episode.
- Per-seat trace (JSONL): frame, seat, controller kind/name, observation hash, legal list hash, chosen input, source (`controller` | `fallback`), latency (from the injectable clock). A `replay(trace)` that re-feeds the recorded inputs and reproduces the final snapshot.
- `swap_seats=True` option for seat-swapped comparisons.
- Human input path: the human controller just returns whatever the frontend queued (or `WAIT`), so `play.py` can use the runner later (not in this task).

Acceptance criteria (failing tests first):
1. Isolation: a controller that tries to mutate its observation cannot affect the episode or the other seat; two controller instances share no state; the controller never receives an object exposing the episode.
2. Fallback: forced exception and forced slow decision each yield a labelled `WAIT` fallback; the other seat keeps acting on its cadence; counts appear in the result.
3. Illegal / malformed answers refused, counted, no state change from the refused action.
4. Determinism: same seed + same scripted controllers → identical trace and outcome; `replay` reproduces the final snapshot.
5. Seat swap produces mirrored seat labels in the result.
6. Full suite green; result line reported. Handoff to QA block.
